Switch finder

Every standard J1939 switch the truck is broadcasting, with its PGN and source address (SA). States update live: press the brake or flip a switch and watch it change.

Detect any switch

Finds every switch on the network, including manufacturer switches that are not in the standard list. Press Start, don't touch anything for 3 seconds, then operate the switches one by one.
